## What stays on your device
Your videos are processed locally. Video decoding, highlight analysis, clip creation and final MP4 rendering happen on the phone. We do not run a cloud video-processing service and do not upload entire videos by default.

AI provider keys and YouTube tokens you enter are encrypted and stored in private app storage. They are never shown again after saving, never logged, and never included in the APK.

## What may be sent online
AI features work only after you configure a provider yourself. A connection test is sent to the provider you choose. If you use AI analysis, only the minimum necessary text, metadata or selected frames are sent, and only to the provider you configured. YouTube upload happens only after you explicitly connect your channel and authorize it.

## Accounts
YouTube sign-in uses Google's official authorization flow. We never ask for your Google password inside the app. Disconnecting removes stored tokens from the device.

## Updates
Updates come from the official GitHub Releases repository and install through Android's own installer. Settings and saved keys stay on your device.
