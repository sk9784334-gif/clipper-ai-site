Clipper AI — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Clipper AI is an on-device video clipping app. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

What stays on your device

Your videos are processed locally on your phone. Video decoding, highlight analysis, clip creation and final MP4 rendering happen on the device. We do not run a cloud video-processing service and we do not upload your entire videos by default.

AI provider keys and YouTube tokens you enter are encrypted and stored in your device's private app storage. They are never shown again after saving, never written to logs, and never included in the app package.

What may be sent online

AI features work only after you configure a provider yourself. A connection test is sent to the provider you choose. If you use AI analysis, only the minimum necessary text, metadata or selected frames are sent, and only to the provider you configured. YouTube upload happens only after you explicitly connect your channel and authorize the upload.

Accounts

YouTube sign-in uses Google's official authorization flow. We never ask for your Google password inside the app. You can disconnect your channel at any time from the app, which removes the stored tokens from your device.

Updates

App updates are downloaded from the official GitHub Releases repository. Installation is always approved through Android's own installer, and your settings and saved keys stay on your device.
