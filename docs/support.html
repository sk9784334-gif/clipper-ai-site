Report a problem

The fastest way is the Report a Problem button inside the app — it opens an email with the details filled in. You can also email us directly with:

  • what you were trying to do
  • what happened instead
  • your phone model and Android version

Common questions

How do I connect an AI provider? Open AI Settings, tap Get API Key, follow the official website, return to the app, paste your key and tap Save & Test Connection.

How do I connect YouTube? Open YouTube, complete Google Setup once with your Client ID, then tap Connect YouTube and choose your account.

Where is my privacy policy? Read the Privacy Policy.
